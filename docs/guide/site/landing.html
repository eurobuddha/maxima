Get started

Pick one. Follow the steps.

Each takes a few minutes. Your 24 words are your identity: write them on paper and keep them safe.

Chat on your Android phone

Android 9 or newer.

  1. On the phone, download the app:
    parlons-{{ANDROID_VERSION}}.apk
  2. Open the download and tap Install. If Android asks, allow installs from your browser.
  3. Open Parlons and tap Create new identity.
  4. Write the 24 words on paper, in order. Tap I've saved them.
  5. Allow notifications, so you see messages arrive.
More detail →

Chat on your computer

Mac (Apple silicon), Windows 10/11, or Linux.

  1. Download Parlons Desktop:
    MacWindowsLinux
  2. Install it. On a Mac, drag MaximaNode into Applications. On Windows, if a blue box appears, click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Open MaximaNode.
  4. Write down the 24 words it shows.
More detail →

One account on all your devices

Same chats on every phone and computer. Needs a Linux machine that stays on: a server or a Raspberry Pi.

  1. On the Linux machine, paste:
    curl -fsSL {{NODE_INSTALL_SH}} | bash
  2. When asked, choose an admin password. Type it again when asked, and a QR code appears.
  3. On your Android phone, install Parlons Cloud:
    parlons-cloud-portal-{{PORTAL_VERSION}}-release.apk
  4. In Parlons Cloud, tap Scan pairing QR, scan the code, then tap Connect & pair.
More detail →

Then add a friend: open Contacts, tap + Add (on a computer, Add contact), scan or paste their address, and tap Introduce myself. They appear when their device answers.
