A network run by its people

Parlons is decentralised: communication runs over the Maxima network, carried by community-operated relays. Choose your relays, run your own node, and talk across the network without a central chat service.

Your conversations stay yours

Messages, voice notes and private files are encrypted end to end. Relays carry the encrypted content; they cannot read your conversations or open your files.

Communicate freely

Exchange text, audio and files with your contacts, and send Minima or MxUSD from wallet-enabled accounts. One conversation for talking, sharing and paying.

Payments are recorded on the public Minima blockchain. End-to-end encryption protects your conversation; it does not make blockchain transactions anonymous. Chat-only guest accounts do not include payments.

Private content. No tracking. Your choice of host.

Parlons has no analytics or advertising trackers. Its relays cannot read your encrypted message content. Your device or Cloud account host holds your readable history and keys, so run your own host or choose one you trust.

Before you start

A few things that make it clearer.

Can I talk to people using a different version?

Yes. The Parlons apps use the same messaging network. Exchange contact addresses, even if you use different apps.

Can I invite friends without sharing my own account?

Yes. Create a separate tenant account on your Parlons Cloud node and give your friend its invitation. They get their own identity, contacts and chats; their invitation does not give them access to your account or node controls. Use owner invitations only for your own devices.

Is a contact QR the same as a pairing invitation?

No. A contact address lets someone message you. A pairing invitation grants a device access to an account. Keep pairing invitations private.

Can I use my 24 words to synchronise two apps?

Use pairing for a shared account. Running the same seed independently on several devices does not synchronise chat history. The guides explain backups and moving an identity.

Why are the version numbers different?

The phone apps, desktop app and servers have separate releases. Choose by purpose and device, not by the biggest version number.

How will Parlons! work on iPhone?

Through Parlons Cloud: the iPhone app will connect to your always-on account, either on your own node or as a tenant on a friend’s node. The iPhone app is not available yet.
